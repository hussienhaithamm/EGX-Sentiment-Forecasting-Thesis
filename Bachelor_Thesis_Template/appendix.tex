\chapter{Reproducibility Notes}

The main runner is \codepath{scripts/run_egx30_5yr_pipeline.py}. It rebuilds the point-in-time registry, prepares processed news, runs the EGX30 sentiment-only evaluation, executes validity checks, generates research summaries, checks thesis artifacts, and compiles the thesis.

The main evaluation and report scripts are listed below:

\begin{pathblock}
\pathrow{scripts/run_egx30_5yr_evaluation.py}
\pathrow{scripts/run_egx30_5yr_validity_checks.py}
\pathrow{scripts/run_easy_win_validity_extensions.py}
\pathrow{scripts/write_egx30_5yr_research_summary.py}
\end{pathblock}

The compact reports are written under \codepath{reports/}.

The central reproducibility command is:
\begin{pathblock}
\pathrow{PYTHONPATH=src python3 scripts/run_egx30_5yr_pipeline.py --skip-scrape}
\end{pathblock}

The \texttt{--skip-scrape} flag reuses the local scrape cache. Removing it resumes the archival scraper, but no new scraping is required to reproduce the reported thesis results from the existing prepared artifacts.
