\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{chap:abstract}

This thesis will study whether bilingual Arabic-English financial news contains short-horizon directional information for the Egyptian Exchange 30-stock benchmark index (EGX30). It will address an emerging-market setting where firm-related news appears in both Arabic and English. It will also avoid survivorship bias from retrospective use of the current EGX30 list.

The study will reconstruct a point-in-time (PIT) EGX30 constituent universe from 21 April 2021 to 20 April 2026. The final registry will contain 12 continuous membership intervals and a historical union of 57 companies. The pipeline will collect, normalize, active-membership filter, deduplicate, and sentiment-score Arabic and English Mubasher articles linked to those constituents.

The primary forecasting design will remain sentiment-only. EGX30 historical closes will construct 1-, 3-, and 5-trading-day direction labels only. Open, High, Low, Close, Volume (OHLCV) fields, returns, technical indicators, and other price-derived variables will stay outside the main model matrix. Daily company sentiment will form an equal-weight market-mood signal for strict expanding-window walk-forward validation.

The results chapter will report the strongest bilingual row by balanced accuracy. That row will use FinBERT plus CAMeLBERT at the 1-trading-day horizon. It will reach accuracy 0.621, balanced accuracy 0.545, F1 0.737, and Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) 0.115. Baseline checks will show that raw accuracy partly reflects majority-class behavior.

A supplementary XGBoost integration experiment will add the same lagged sentiment features to an EGX30 historical OHLCV model. Accuracy will improve from 0.739 to 0.802, while balanced accuracy will improve from 0.757 to 0.793. A validity audit will check target recomputation, lag alignment, point-in-time membership, cross-company deduplication, walk-forward history, and metric parity.

The thesis will provide limited evidence of weak directional signal in bilingual EGX30 market mood. Its contribution will be a reproducible and conservative protocol for testing bilingual market mood in an emerging market. It will not present the system as a deployable trading model.
