# Methodology

The production benchmark reconstructs the historical EGX30 universe without survivorship bias, scrapes bilingual company-linked news, filters articles against active membership on publication date, deduplicates stories across company pages, averages firm mood equally across active constituents, shifts every daily feature by at least one calendar day, and evaluates 1, 3, and 5 trading-day EGX30 direction under embargoed expanding-window walk-forward validation. Historical prices are labels only.
